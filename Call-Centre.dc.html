More about Vicidial.

QUESTIONS WE GET
What is Vicidial?

The world's most popular open-source call centre platform — auto-dial and click-to-dial campaigns, recording, wallboards, reporting and agent management in one suite. For most call centre requirements it is also the most affordable complete solution.

Is it hosted in the cloud or on-site?

Either. We deploy Vicidial anywhere in the world — in our own high-throughput voice data centre, in your cloud, or on-site — as a fully managed or unmanaged service.

Can it be customised and integrated with our systems?

Yes — that is our speciality. We modify Vicidial, develop new features for it, and integrate it with CRMs and other third-party applications to further enhance business efficiency.

How far does it scale?

Further than most floors will ever need. Our largest managed deployment ran a single 50-server cluster supporting over 4 000 concurrent agents.

What does it cost?

Standard service from R345 per user per month. Premium from R555 per user adds our super-low 9c per minute rate to Vodacom and MTN — with equally low rates to all other destinations, billed per second.

We already run our own Vicidial — can you still help?

Yes. We offer consultation and service-level agreements for teams that maintain their own instance — advice and backup you can rely on, without handing over the keys.
