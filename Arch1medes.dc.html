AVM · dialler · WebRTC · media streaming

One engine, dialling at scale.

AVM is what Arch1medes was built for in the first place: contacting millions of subscribers a day with voice broadcasts, surveys, reminders and alerts — and it is far from all the engine does. The same core is a predictive dialler for call centres, exposing a full suite of call centre telephony over API, serving WebRTC extensions to agents through that same suite, and streaming media both ways for teams building AI voice agents.

1,5s voicemail detection Voice AI dynamic CLIs multi-tenant + 2FA API-first
ROLE 01 · AUTOMATIC VOICE MESSAGING

Reach a nation in a day.

Broadcasts, surveys, reminders and alerts placed by the million, with the engine pacing itself against each operator and detecting voicemail before it wastes a channel. Answers are handed straight into the workflow that needs them — an IVR question set, a callback queue or a live agent.

✓Millions of outbound calls a day from one engine ✓Voicemail detection tunable per operator and campaign ✓Dynamic CLI selection, including mobile presentation ✓Per-recipient data merged into TTS and IVR prompts ✓Retry and pacing rules that protect answer rates ✓Every leg written back as a live event stream
ROLE 02 · CALL CENTRE SUITE VIA API

A whole call centre, exposed as an API.

For software teams that need call centre telephony at scale inside their own product. Predictive and preview dialling, blended queues, dispositions, recordings and live statistics are all driven over REST, ESL and webhooks — and agents get a WebRTC extension served from the same suite, so there is no softphone to deploy.

✓Predictive, progressive and preview dialling over API ✓Blended inbound and outbound queues with skills routing ✓WebRTC extensions served to agents in the browser ✓Dispositions, recordings and CDRs available immediately ✓Live floor statistics streamed rather than polled ✓Multi-tenant by design, white-labelled where you need it
ROLE 03 · NATIVE VOICE AI

Let the AI take the mundane calls.

Bidirectional media streaming is native to the engine, so an AI agent can sit on a call exactly where a human would. Point the repetitive work at the model — confirmations, qualification, balance queries, first-line triage — and keep your people on the calls where judgement and revenue actually live. The handover carries full context, in either direction.

✓Bidirectional audio streams per call leg, natively ✓AI agents and humans share one queue and one report ✓Warm handover to a person with the transcript attached ✓Sub-second first response on live calls ✓Barge-in, DTMF and silence handling done in the engine ✓Scale AI coverage campaign by campaign, not all at once

QUESTIONS WE GET
Is it a dialler or a platform?

Both. The dialler is the visible part; underneath is a telephony engine with the whole call centre suite exposed by API.

How far does it scale?

There is no theoretical ceiling in the software. Throughput is a function of the hardware you give it.

Can we run it under our own brand?

Yes. Multi-tenancy is native, with 2FA security, so you can resell capacity to your own clients.

Can it work with our existing systems?

Every function is available by API, and workflows can call your systems the moment a call outcome lands.
