TELEPHONY CONSULTING

Get the voice layer right before you scale it.

Most voice problems are architecture problems wearing a carrier’s uniform: trunks sized by guesswork, routing nobody has read in years, dial plans grown by accretion. We review what you carry, model where it breaks and re-specify the parts that matter — from a single office PBX to a contact centre running thousands of concurrent calls.

QUESTIONS WE GET
Do we have to buy your services afterwards?

No. The engagement is the advice; if the right answer is your existing carrier, your existing platform or a competitor’s product, that is what the report will say.

Can you review something we have already built?

Yes — that is most of this work. We audit what is running, quantify where it will break and give you an ordered list of what to fix first.

Will you work alongside our own team or vendor?

Routinely. We can advise, oversee implementation, or transfer the knowledge so your team carries it from there.

How long does a review take?

Small reviews are days; a full telephony, cloud and software assessment is typically two to four weeks depending on how much traffic and code there is to read.
