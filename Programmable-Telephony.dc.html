QUESTIONS WE GET
Asterisk or Freeswitch — which one should we use?

It depends on the workload. Freeswitch handles high-capacity media and multi-tenancy more efficiently; Asterisk has the wider ecosystem and is often the pragmatic choice where integrations already exist. We will tell you which one your project actually needs, including when the answer is the one you are already running.

Do you work with our developers or replace them?

We work with them. The point of the engagement is that your team ends up able to run and extend the integration themselves — pairing, code review and written runbooks are part of the deliverable.

What does the SLA actually cover?

The heavy lifting: version upgrades, end-of-life migrations, regression testing against your own call flows, real-time monitoring of your systems, and named engineers who already know your deployment. Knowledge transfer is included rather than billed as a workshop.

Must it be hosted with you?

No. We prefer it — our Teraco environment was built for real-time voice — but the same containerised deployment runs on your hardware or any cloud region you need, including hybrid splits where media stays local.

Can you take over an integration somebody else built?

Regularly. We audit it, stabilise it, then evolve it. A rewrite is a last resort, not an opening position.
