QUESTIONS WE GET
What is the difference between reseller and agent?

A reseller owns the customer, the brand and first-line support at wholesale rates. An agent introduces the lead, we take it from there, and commission is paid for 24 months.

Do my clients ever find out about you?

Not on the reseller model, unless you want them to. Some resellers prefer to leverage our name, the option is yours. The service, billing and support all carry your brand; we sit behind you as the engine room.

What support do I get?

Second and third line engineering support at a 30 minute average response, from the same team that runs our own platforms. We also offer telephone support for emergencies.

Is there a minimum volume?

No hard minimum — what we look for is customers. Any registered company with an existing base can resell. And if you have no base yet but do have a client waiting on a quote, that counts too: come and talk to us.
